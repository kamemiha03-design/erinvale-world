# Playing Erinvale

## Controls

### iPhone / touch
- Left virtual stick: walk forward, backward and sideways.
- Drag the right side of the world: look around.
- 「調べる」: inspect nearby landmarks.

### Keyboard / mouse
- W A S D or arrow keys: walk.
- Click the world to capture the mouse; move the mouse to look around.
- E: inspect the nearby landmark.
- Esc: release the mouse.

## Current playable area

The first playable build contains the royal plaza, Copper Stag tavern, adventurers' guild, market stalls, imperial fountain, royal gatehouse, distant castle silhouette and the World Tree. The tavern and guild have walk-through entrances and simple furnished interiors.

This is an early playable exploration build, not the final art-quality release. The detailed production pass (custom GLB architecture, scanned PBR surfaces, natural water and foliage animation, spatial ambience and additional regions) remains in progress.
